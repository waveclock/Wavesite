Meet Buddy

A friendly recurring character who lives on your Custom screen -- and whose mood matches today's real tide and weather, right where you live.

No app, nothing to publish, nothing to keep up with. Turn Buddy on once from Custom Screen → Beach Buddy in the design tool, and he takes it from there: same friendly stick-figure guy every day, drawn fresh by AI, doing whatever today's actual conditions call for.

How it works

Buddy checks the real conditions, then acts them out

Every hour, Buddy looks at the real tide, wind, waves, and sky for your exact town -- the same data your Tide screen already uses -- and picks whichever of these actually fits today. The headline always shows the real specific detail (the tide time, the wind speed, the swell height) -- never made up.

  • Rain today? Buddy's got his umbrella up.
  • Windy? He's out flying a kite.
  • Big swell? He's up and riding it.
  • Calm, small waves? Paddleboard day.
  • A high or low tide coming up during the day? He calls it out, right down to the time.
  • Sunrise or sunset happening right now? He's watching it -- sunset from the end of a bayside dock.
  • After dark? He's stargazing, under tonight's real moon phase.
  • Great fishing conditions? He's out on the jetty with a rod.
  • Otherwise, just a perfect calm day? Buddy mixes it up -- lounging under his umbrella, riding his beach cruiser along the boardwalk, or grabbing coffee and donuts -- so it's not the same scene every calm day.
Clear skies get their own little touch too: on a mostly-cloudless day (or a genuinely clear night), Buddy's scene gets a big sun overhead -- or, after dark, a callout that it's a great night for stargazing.
