Why it looks so enormous

The "moon illusion" is strongest right at the horizon — and Ocean City's flat, open beach-to-ocean sightline is about as good a stage for it as you'll find on the East Coast.

The horizon illusion

A moon low on the horizon appears far larger than the same moon overhead — a trick of the brain, not the eyes. Ocean City's unobstructed water horizon makes that illusion as dramatic as it gets.

Perfect timing tonight

Moonrise landing just before sunset means the moon climbs into a still-bright sky, framed by real detail on the horizon instead of pure darkness.

The boardwalk frame

Standing back from the beach lines the Music Pier and boardwalk silhouettes right up against the rising disk — a shot you can only get from this stretch of coast.

A rare double-feature

Pairing a Full Moon moonrise with a same-night eclipse this deep (96%) doesn't happen often — tonight's the night.
