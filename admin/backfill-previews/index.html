Backfill Missing Previews

PNG previews weren't always generated alongside the firmware .bin -- this finds every published Custom Screen that has a .bin but no matching .png, and regenerates the .png straight from the .bin's own pixel data.

Read-only until you click a button: nothing is downloaded or written until you press "Generate" on a row, or "Generate all" below. Only ever creates the missing .png -- never touches the .bin or anything already published.
