Why it looks so enormous

The "moon illusion" is strongest right at the horizon — and Ocean City's flat, open beach-to-ocean sightline is about as good a stage for it as you'll find on the East Coast.

The horizon illusion

A moon low on the horizon appears far larger than the same moon overhead — a trick of the brain, not the eyes. Ocean City's unobstructed water horizon makes that illusion as dramatic as it gets.

It's a moving target

Moonrise shifts by about 50 minutes later each night, and its rise direction swings across the compass over the month — so where and when to look is always changing.
